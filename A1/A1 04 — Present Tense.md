Greek normally drops the subject pronoun. **Μένω στη Λευκωσία.** — I live in Nicosia. Use a pronoun for emphasis or contrast: **Εγώ μένω εδώ, κι εσύ;** — I live here. How about you?

Greek uses the same present form for English *I work* and *I am working*: **Δουλεύω κάθε μέρα. Δουλεύω τώρα.** Use **εσείς** and the corresponding verb ending for more than one person or one person addressed politely.

Practise **Πάω στο σπίτι** (I'm going home), **Θέλω να φάω** (I want to eat), **Μου αρέσει ο καφές** (I like coffee) and **Μου αρέσουν τα βιβλία** (I like books). With **αρέσει / αρέσουν**, the thing you like determines the verb form.

Say where you live, what languages you speak and what you like.
