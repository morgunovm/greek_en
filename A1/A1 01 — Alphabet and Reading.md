# Alphabet and reading

| Letter | Approximate sound | Letter | Approximate sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Α α | a as in father | Ν ν | n |
| Β β | v | Ξ ξ | ks |
| Γ γ | voiced counterpart of the χ sound, softened to a y-like sound before e or i | Ο ο | short, pure o |
| Δ δ | th as in this | Π π | p |
| Ε ε | e as in bed | Ρ ρ | tapped or rolled r |
| Ζ ζ | z | Σ σ/ς | s |
| Η η | ee as in see | Τ τ | t |
| Θ θ | th as in think | Υ υ | ee as in see |
| Ι ι | ee as in see | Φ φ | f |
| Κ κ | k | Χ χ | ch in Scottish loch, softer before e or i |
| Λ λ | l | Ψ ψ | ps |
| Μ μ | m | Ω ω | same as ο |

**ς** is the form of **σ** used at the end of a word. The accent mark shows which syllable to stress. The Greek question mark looks like an English semicolon: **Πού μένεις;**

| Spelling | Sound | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| αι | e | και |
| ει, οι, υι | ee | είμαι, οικογένεια, υιός |
| ου | oo as in food | μου |
| αυ, ευ | av/ev before vowels and voiced sounds, af/ef before voiceless sounds | αύριο, ευχαριστώ |
| μπ, ντ, γκ | b, d, g at the start of a word, sometimes with a nasal sound within a word | μπάλα, ντομάτα, γκολ |
| γγ | usually ng as in finger, softened before e or i | άγγελος |
| τσ, τζ | ts, dz | τσάι, τζάμι |

Two dots can show that adjacent vowels are pronounced separately, as in **Μαΐου**. Read **νερό, παιδί, φίλος, όχι, σχολείο** aloud and identify the stressed syllable.
