## How to study

1. Read one lesson and say the Greek examples aloud.
2. Pick 10–15 items from a related vocabulary topic. Learn nouns with their articles and practise verbs in short sentences.
3. Try the [[A1 — Practice|A1 exercises]] or [[A2 — Practice|A2 exercises]]. Click to reveal the answers.
4. Review the items in both directions after a day, a week and a month.
